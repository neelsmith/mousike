Mousike Notation

A font for ancient Greek musical notation as encoded in Zach Sowerby's URN scheme, made for the mousike Python package. It covers every vocal and instrumental sign in the encoding's table, and positions the combining length signs and arsis dot that mousike writes for each note.
Download the font (desktop TTF, web WOFF2, licence and this specimen): github.com/neelsmith/mousike/releases/latest/download/MousikeNotation.zip

Combining marks

disemeΖ̅U+0305COMBINING OVERLINE
trisemeΖ𝉂U+1D242COMBINING GREEK MUSICAL TRISEME
tetrasemeΖ𝉃U+1D243COMBINING GREEK MUSICAL TETRASEME
pentasemeΖ𝉄U+1D244COMBINING GREEK MUSICAL PENTASEME
arsis (stigme)Ζ̇U+0307COMBINING DOT ABOVE
length + arsisΖ𝉂̇e.g. U+1D242 U+0307the dot stacks above the length sign
Each sign below is shown in four forms: plain · diseme · triseme + arsis · pentaseme.
